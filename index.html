// questions

Common questions

Do you only work with clients in Zamboanga City?

No — I'm based in Zamboanga City and happy to meet locally, but I work remotely with clients anywhere in the Philippines. Most projects run fully online.

How much does a system cost?

It depends on scope, so I quote per project after we talk — a simple website and a full operations platform are very different builds. The quote is fixed before work starts.

Who owns the system when it's done?

You do. Code, data, and accounts are handed over to you at launch.

What if we already have a system that's half-working?

That's common. I can audit what exists, fix what's broken, and extend it — you don't have to start from zero.

Are you a developer or an agency?

One developer — the person you message is the person who builds. I studied Computer Science at WMSU, hold a Lean Six Sigma White Belt, took executive education in Digital Transformation at the Asian Institute of Management, and build daily at VINTAZK Outsourcing.
